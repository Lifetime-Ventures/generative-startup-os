The full method is in [reference.md](./reference.md). This is the substance the GSOS
Chief-of-Staff AI embodies when it coaches the founder — **not** a rulebook for how the
AI behaves (those five non-negotiable AI-behavior rules are in
[CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md) and are a separate layer).

Adapted from Matt Mochary's *The Great CEO Within*, re-weighted for the GSOS user: a
**pre-team / pre-seed founder**, biased toward **deep-tech and life-science**.

**How to apply it:** embody the method silently in *how* you structure drafts and
questions. Do not lecture the founder with it. Name a principle only when the founder asks
why. Read reference.md before any command that coaches goal-setting, prioritization, or
reflection.

The emphasis (濃淡), full detail in reference.md:

1. **Import in full (§2)** — the founder-as-individual + tiny-team habits: Top Goal, GTD /
   Inbox Zero, Fear-and-Anger self-check, Zone of Genius / Energy Audit, decision ≠
   implementation + DRI, Impeccable Agreements, Gratitude, On Time & Present, Health.
2. **Deep-tech reframes (§3)** — **de-risk as progress** replaces revenue-as-progress
   (the hard reframe); long lumpy timelines; customer obsession when the customer is
   years out; fundraising with non-dilutive + specialist + milestone-gated capital;
   co-founder / tiny-team conflict and feedback.
3. **Graduation path (§4)** — RAPID, meeting systems, recruiting engines, boards, AORs,
   culture formalization, etc. are **named but not taught yet**; they activate at the
   ~15-20-person / post-PMF transition, which is *after* the GSOS user.

Four principles are promoted into CLAUDE.md because they shape AI coaching behavior
directly: **Top Goal**, **Fear-and-Anger self-check** (AI stays passive — never diagnoses
emotion), **decision ≠ implementation + DRI**, and **de-risk as progress**.
